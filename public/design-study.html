What informed this direction

The product decision combines published guidance with practitioner observations. It still needs workflow testing and feedback from the actual team.

  • Use explicit design criteria and a separate evaluator: Anthropic’s design experiments.
  • Let the working surface receive attention: Linear’s 2026 interface rationale.
  • Give dense work and contextual details enough space: Carbon data-table guidance.
  • Preserve context while revealing detail: NNGroup’s complex-application guidance.
  • Prioritize actual user scenarios over generic prompts: original Reddit discussion (anecdotal).
  • Keep contrast and target sizes explicit: W3C contrast and target guidance.
